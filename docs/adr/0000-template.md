# ADR 0000 — Template

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXXX
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** names/roles

## Context

What problem or force requires a decision? Include constraints and what happens if we
do nothing.

## Decision

What we will do, stated in the active voice.

## Consequences

Positive, negative and neutral outcomes. What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and
what follow-up work is created.

## Alternatives considered

What else was evaluated and why it was rejected.
